# Privacy Policy — Smart Shopping List

Effective 13 November 2026 · Version 1.0

**Developer:** quiteusefulapp ("Developer")
**Privacy contact:** quiteusefulapp@gmail.com

## 1. Summary
Smart Shopping List (also named "Умный список покупок" and "Smart Tizim" in some languages; the "App") runs on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. **The Developer does not collect, receive, store, sell or share your personal data.**

## 2. What data is in the App and where it lives
Everything you enter (lists, items, quantities, prices, budgets, dishes, purchase history, dates and reminders, settings) is stored **only on your device**, in the App's own database. The Developer has no access to it.
It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → the App → Storage → Clear data). Backup files you saved yourself must be deleted by you.

## 3. Permissions and why
- **Notifications and exact alarms** — to show the shopping reminders you set, at the time you choose. Reminders are created and run on the device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Microphone / speech recognition** (only when you tap the voice-input button) — to turn speech into item text. Recognition is performed by your phone's speech-recognition service, which on many devices is provided by Google and depends on your Android settings. That service is separate from the Developer and is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. The Developer does not receive the audio or the recognized text. Voice input is optional; every feature works with the keyboard.
The App does **not** request access to contacts, location, camera, photos, files, accounts, SMS or calls.

## 4. Internet
The Google Play version of the App **does not send data over the internet** and contains no advertising or analytics libraries. The only exceptions are the external speech-recognition service (section 3) if you use it, and links you open in your browser yourself.

## 5. Sharing
The Developer shares nothing with third parties. Data leaves your device only when you act: when you tap "Share" or "Save backup", or send a list file or text through an app you choose (messenger, email, cloud). From then on, that app is responsible.

## 6. Children
The App is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent data to the Developer, write to the address above.

## 7. Security
Data is kept in the App's protected storage on your device; device security (screen lock, Android updates, backups) is up to you. A backup file you save is not encrypted — store and send it with care.

## 8. Your rights
Because the Developer does not process your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the Developer's side: you control your data yourself (edit and delete lists, clear the App's data). If you believe the Developer has nevertheless received your data, write to the address above — we will reply within a reasonable time (up to 30 days), tell you what we hold and delete it on request. You may also contact the data-protection authority in your country. This policy follows the general principles of the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Personal Data and Their Protection" (No. 94-V of 21 May 2013) and of the GDPR (data minimisation, transparency, on-device storage).

## 9. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and shown in the App, and the date above will change. A new App version will not start collecting data without a separate clear notice and your consent.
